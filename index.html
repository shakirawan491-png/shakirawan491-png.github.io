Expertise

Entomology & Plant Protection

Insect pests

  • Sucking: aphids, whiteflies, thrips, jassids, psyllids, mealybugs, scale insects, spider mites, dubas bug
  • Chewing: armyworm, cutworm, leaf miners, caterpillars, grasshoppers, leaf beetles
  • Palm: red palm weevil, rhinoceros beetle, longhorn stem borer, date palm mites
  • Soil & turf: white grubs, mole crickets, termites, ants, nematodes

Plant diseases

  • Fungal: powdery and downy mildew, rust, anthracnose, blight, damping-off, root and crown rot, leaf spots
  • Turf: dollar spot, brown patch, Pythium blight, rust
  • Palm: black scorch, Fusarium wilt, Graphiola leaf spot, bud rot, heart rot
  • Bacterial & viral: bacterial blight, soft rot, crown gall, mosaic viruses
  • Abiotic: nutrient deficiency, chlorosis, salinity, sunburn, water stress

Control & monitoring

  • Pest and disease identification from symptoms, damage patterns and life cycles
  • Field scouting, sampling and economic threshold assessment
  • Pheromone, sticky, light and pitfall traps
  • Biological control: ladybird beetles, lacewings, parasitoid wasps, predatory mites, Beauveria bassiana, neem

Safe pesticide use

  • Insecticide, fungicide, acaricide and herbicide use
  • Sprayer calibration and resistance management
  • PPE, re-entry and pre-harvest intervals, record keeping
  • Sample collection, pest and disease reports, treatment recommendations
